Behavioral interview questions

An interview-prep tool: select the principle you want to test, get example questions and guidance.

If you already know a listed company's principles, pick that company and use those names. Or type the principle you care about in your own words.

The over 200 behavioral interview questions in this tool are the questions I have used and collected over the years. The examples provided were generated based on dozens of real interview notes and debriefs that I anonymized and fed into an LLM with a tuned prompt.

Each question includes example answers showing a hire-level answer and a common no-hire answer.
